# CS-255-System-Analysis-and-Design
My coursework and projects from CS-255 System Analysis and Design at SNHU.

DriverPass Project Reflection

1. What was the DriverPass project?

DriverPass wanted a system to help students prepare for their driving tests. Students needed to register, buy training packages, take online classes and practice tests, and schedule driving lessons. Employees also needed a way to manage appointments and student information.

2. What did I do well?

I think I did well organizing the customer's requirements and figuring out what the system needed to do. I also worked on creating diagrams that showed how students and employees would use the system.

3. What would I improve?

I would improve my UML diagrams. Some of the connecting lines were hard to organize, so I would spend more time making everything easier to read.

4. How did I include the user's needs?

I used the client interview to understand what DriverPass needed. I considered what students, employees, and the owner would need from the system. This matters because software should make things easier for the people using it.

5. How do I approach software design?

I start by understanding what the customer wants. Then I organize the requirements and plan how the system should work. In the future, I would continue using UML diagrams and checking my designs before moving into development.
